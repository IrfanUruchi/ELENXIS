# Research status and limitations

**Status: experimental; independent scientific review pending.** The internal scientific evidence gate remains closed.

Internal controlled work has included typed input validation, source-integrity checks, documentary lineage reasoning, numerical uncertainty and covariance cases, and targeted adversarial source-identity tests. These show progress on bounded engineering objectives; they do **not** establish broad scientific accuracy, universal source authentication or suitability for autonomous decision-making.

A scoped source-binding experiment preserved the original documentary classifications and rejected previously successful synthetic input-binding forgeries after hardening. This was a bounded regression test relying on a trusted reference, not a general security certification.

There is no claim of independent experimental replication from multiple representations of one publication. There is no public release of the private engine or complete research archives.

**Restricted uses:** the public overview must not be used to make scientific, medical, regulatory, engineering-safety, legal or financial decisions. Any future evaluation requires explicit scope, human review and an appropriate agreement.
